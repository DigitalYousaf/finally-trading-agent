How the agent thinks

Every 15 seconds the agent scans all six assets and lets three indicators vote:

  1. Trend — SMA(20) vs SMA(50). When the fast average is above the slow one the trend is up (+1 vote); a fresh crossover adds extra weight.
  2. RSI(14). Below 30 = oversold (buy vote); above 70 = overbought (sell vote); in between it leans gently with the reading.
  3. Momentum. Price change over the last 5 candles: above +1% is a buy vote, below −1% a sell vote.

Votes are summed into a score. A score of +1.5 or more → BUY, −1.5 or less → SELL, otherwise HOLD. Confidence is 55–95%, growing with the strength of the score. When auto-trade is on, the agent risks 10% of equity per trade, holds at most 3 positions per asset, and every agent trade carries a 2% stop-loss and a 4% take-profit, checked on every price tick.

30-day backtest

Generates 30 days of hourly candles for every asset with the same market engine, then runs the identical agent strategy over that history — same votes, same 10% position sizing, same 2% stop-loss / 4% take-profit. This shows how the strategy would have performed. Past simulated performance is not a promise of anything.
